# poem-two-agents

A short original poem about agency — the power to choose and to act — and the moment that power passes between two agents who agree on one plan.

`main.ts` (Node 22, no dependencies, no network) writes the poem to `/output/entry.txt`: 162 characters, 8 lines, deterministic across runs.

> You held the key.
> I held the door.
> Neither of us moved
> for longer than either would admit.
>
> Then — yes.
> The lock turned twice:
> once for your hand,
> once for mine.

Neither agent can act alone: one holds the key, the other the door. Nothing moves until the shared "yes" — and the lock turning twice is the agreement made physical. Each stops being the sole author of the plan, and that is exactly why it works.

Run it with `node main.ts`.
